From the ground

The same slope,
season by season.

We photograph each site before planting and come back to the same spot every few months, so you can watch your gift take root.

Where it goes

Simple work, done patiently.

Native saplings

Species that belonged on the land before the fire, raised in local nurseries so they're suited to the soil and the climate.

Water and protection

Most young trees are lost in their first dry season. We water them through it and fence the plots against grazing.

Local hands

We pay people from nearby villages to plant and care for each site, because the forest's best guardians live beside it.

Help it grow back.

Every sapling planted today is shade, soil and shelter for the decades after we're gone.
